## Learning goal

Solve one-variable linear equations for homework: one-step equations
(a single addition/subtraction or multiplication/division standing between
the learner and `x`) and two-step equations (two operations to undo, in the
right order), and check answers by substitution.

Narrowed from the learner's original broad statement ("get better at
algebra, solving equations") by asking what kind of equations show up on
their homework — they described single-variable equations, sometimes with
terms on both sides, nothing about systems or quadratics. Scope kept to
one-step and two-step for now; variables-on-both-sides can be a follow-up
goal once this one is met (YAGNI on scope).

## Observable mastery criteria

- Given an unseen one-step equation (e.g. `x - 8 = 15`), solves for `x`
  correctly, unaided.
- Given an unseen two-step equation (e.g. `4x + 7 = 23`), solves for `x`
  correctly, showing the operations undone in the right order, unaided.
- Checks a solution by substituting it back into the original equation and
  confirming both sides are equal, for at least 3 unseen equations.

Confirmed with the learner in one sentence: "So the goal is: you can solve
one-step and two-step equations for x on your own, and check your answer
by plugging it back in — sound right?" Learner agreed.
